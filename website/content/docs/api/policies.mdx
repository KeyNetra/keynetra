# Policies

Policies in KeyNetra are the durable source of truth for authorization behavior outside simple role or relationship resolution.

## Typical lifecycle

1. Author a policy definition.
2. Validate and lint before rollout.
3. Simulate impact against representative requests.
4. Publish a revision.
5. Audit live decisions after release.

## Why the lifecycle matters

Authorization changes are high leverage. Treating them like deployable artifacts reduces regressions and makes rollbacks practical.
