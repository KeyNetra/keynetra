# RBAC in KeyNetra

RBAC is the fastest way to centralize broad application permissions. Roles become part of the authorization model instead of becoming switch statements spread across handlers.

## When RBAC fits

- Internal tools with job-function permissions
- SaaS admin consoles
- Tenant-level plans or entitlement tiers

## Common failure mode

Role explosion happens when teams use roles to encode every exception. Once that starts, roles stop being durable abstractions and become serialized business logic.

## KeyNetra approach

Use RBAC for stable access tiers and move exceptions to policy or relationship edges. That keeps roles legible and easy to audit.
