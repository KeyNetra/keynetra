# Why authorization is hard

Authentication answers who a user is. Authorization answers what they can do, on which resource, under what conditions, and why that answer should still hold after the next product change.

## Product structure leaks into authorization

As soon as a product supports organizations, shared resources, delegated ownership, support access, or time-bound exceptions, authorization stops being a simple role lookup.

## Teams underestimate change

The first version of a permission system rarely survives contact with real customers. New features create exceptions. Exceptions create duplicated logic. Duplicated logic creates inconsistency.

## Infrastructure is the right abstraction

Authorization belongs in infrastructure when:

- multiple services need consistent decisions
- policy changes should not require broad redeploys
- support, security, and compliance teams need reliable audit trails
