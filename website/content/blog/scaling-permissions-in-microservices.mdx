# Scaling permissions in microservices

Microservices make access control harder for the same reason they make everything else harder: behavior is distributed.

## Drift happens slowly, then all at once

One service checks a role. Another service checks a role and a feature flag. A third service adds a local override table. The product still appears to work until support has to explain inconsistent denials to customers.

## Centralized decisions reduce ambiguity

A shared authorization layer gives services one place to ask the question, one policy surface to update, and one audit trail to inspect.

## Platform teams need leverage

The goal is not to hide authorization from application engineers. The goal is to give them an interface that is consistent enough to scale.
